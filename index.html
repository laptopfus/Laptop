About Me

Hello! I am a Technical Designer passionate about creating video games.
As a Senior Technical Designer I am involved in almost every step of game production. From game design on the paper to implementation of ideas created to engine itself. During that process I need to cooperate with every department like programmers, level designers, artists, animators, sound.

Current responsibilities:

  • Scripting.
  • Design.
  • Documentation.
  • Behaviour of NPC.
  • Simple scripts in C#.
  • Getting new skills in Python for simple scripts.
  • Providing critical and constructive feedback for Level/Quest designers about their scripting.
  • Providing new LDI, including their design and implementation.
  • Mentoring juniors to develop their skills as fast as possible
  • Conceptualizing and evaluating game design ideas with others.
  • Working with artists, programmers, animators, audio engineers, and other designers to drive the quality and vision of the game while ensuring deliverable dates are met.
  • Tweaking behavior, player abilities, and other gameplay elements to shape and define the core experience.

Known game engines (and projects created):

  • Ubisoft Annecy - Snowdrop (Star Wars: Outlaws), Anvil (Riders Republic, Steep).
  • Flying Wild Hog - Road Hog engine (Shadow Warrior 2).
  • CryEngine 3, CryEngine 2 (Enemy Front, Sniper: Ghost Warrior 2).
  • ChromeEd 4, ChromeEd 2 (Sniper: Ghost Warrior).
  • UDK (Alien Rage)
  • JupiterEX (Budget PC Shooters)

In my free time, having fun with Unreal 5.
